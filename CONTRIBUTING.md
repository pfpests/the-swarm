# Contributing to the swarm 🪰

Welcome to the burrow. This repo is PFP's open door: the ecosystem is built in
public, and collaborations start here.

## Ways in
- **Play first.** The six cabinets of the SWARM ARCADE live on Farcaster
  (pfpests.xyz). Every good idea here starts by playing.
- **Issues** — bugs, cabinet ideas, balance feedback, typos in the wild.
  Screenshots and steps to reproduce make a bug real.
- **Pull requests** — fork, branch, keep it small, say what you tested.
  One idea per PR lands faster than a swarm-sized one.
- **Art & memes** — open an issue with the piece attached. If a pest appears,
  it must be drawn from its official character sheet: character consistency
  is sacred in this house.

## House rules (non-negotiable)
1. **No promises.** Nothing here promises returns, profits or gains — ever.
   Paper is labelled PAPER, simulation is labelled SIMULATION.
2. **Receipts over claims.** If it is not live or verifiable, it is not
   presented as live or verified.
3. **Fun, never cruel.** The swarm teases; it does not punch down.
4. **Cutre in the meme, professional in everything else.**
